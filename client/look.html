Each view is one fixed picture of the game. Drop pictures of what it should look like on the left, say what to take from each, shoot, and compare. When a view looks right, mark it right; when it doesn't, say what's wrong. Your words are the bar: an agent working on the look stops when a view is marked right. The numbers under each picture are luma (whole frame, the sky band at the top, the near band at the bottom) and chroma, 0 to 255, read the same way on both sides.
